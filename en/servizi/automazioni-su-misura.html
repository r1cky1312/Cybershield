What can be automated

Some typical ideas for shops, venues, studios and offices. If your case is different, message us: we'll look at it together.

Automatic data backup

Scheduled copies of important folders and files to an external drive or the cloud, with an alert if a copy fails.

🗂️

File and report management

Automatic organisation of folders and documents, and a summary ready every week from Excel or CSV files, without copying numbers by hand.

Network monitoring

Regular checks on your router, printer, NAS or website: if something stops responding you get an alert, instead of finding out when you need it.

🔁

Small repetitive tasks

Renaming, converting or merging files, filling in templates, preparing PDFs: anything you repeat the same way every day is a good candidate.
