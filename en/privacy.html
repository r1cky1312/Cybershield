Privacy policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data we collect when you contact us or use our services, why we process it and what rights you have. It is drawn up in accordance with EU Regulation 2016/679 (GDPR) and Italian Legislative Decree 196/2003. This is a translation: in case of discrepancies, the Italian version prevails.

1. Data controller

The data controller is CyberShield, represented by Riccardo Apicella, based in Turin, Italy.

  • Email: infocybershield5@gmail.com
  • Phone / WhatsApp: +39 379 287 0887

2. What data we collect

  • Contact details you give us when you message us on WhatsApp, by email or ask for a quote: name, phone number, email address, business name and what you write in your messages.
  • Data about the website to be analysed when you request the free audit: the website address and the public technical information that comes with it.
  • Anonymous browsing data collected in aggregate form by the website's analytics tools (see section 7).

We do not collect special categories of data (health, opinions, etc.) and we do not knowingly process data of minors.

3. Why we process data (purposes) and legal basis

  • Answering your requests and providing quotes or the free audit — legal basis: pre-contractual measures at your request (Art. 6.1.b GDPR).
  • Delivering the agreed services and issuing the related documents — legal basis: performance of the contract (Art. 6.1.b) and legal obligations (Art. 6.1.c).
  • Improving the website through anonymous statistics — legal basis: legitimate interest (Art. 6.1.f).

4. How long we keep data

  • Messages and requests with no contractual follow-up: up to 24 months, then deleted.
  • Data related to services provided and tax documents: for the period required by law (normally 10 years for accounting records).

5. Who data may be shared with

We do not sell your data. It may be processed, only for the purposes above, by the providers that allow us to work, acting as processors or independent controllers:

  • Google (Gmail) — email;
  • Meta / WhatsApp — messaging, when you write to us there;
  • PayPal — if you choose to pay online;
  • Website hosting and analytics provider (e.g. Vercel / GitHub).

Some of these providers may also process data outside the European Union, with the safeguards provided by Arts. 44-49 GDPR.

6. Your rights

At any time you can ask us to: access your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to its processing, and receive your data in a portable format (Arts. 15-22 GDPR). You can also lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority (Garante).

To exercise these rights, write to us at infocybershield5@gmail.com: we reply within 30 days.

7. Cookies and statistics

This website does not use profiling or advertising cookies and does not track your identity. We use a web analytics tool in anonymous, aggregate form (visit counts), which does not require your consent because it does not identify you. If in future we add tools that use non-technical cookies, we will ask for your consent first through a dedicated banner.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect new regulations or the services we offer. The version in force is always the one published on this page, with the date at the top.
